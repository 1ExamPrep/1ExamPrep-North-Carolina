What does the North Carolina Elevator license cover?

The SP-EL special restricted license covers elevator electrical work. Elevator candidates study NEC Article 620 and the ASME elevator safety code.

Which exams do Elevator candidates take?

The State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors gives its own written exams. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current statutes, board rules and board pages.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
Board Elevator (SP-EL) examNEC lookups, calculations and North Carolina electrical rulesWritten exam given by the electrical board

What do the electrical license classes allow?

G.S. 87-43.3 sets three electrical contracting classes and lets the Board create special restricted classes for one phase of work. The Board sets the working project limits, and the statute caps them.

ClassSingle project limitExperience (G.S. 87-43.3)
LimitedUp to the Board limit, capped at $100,000, 1,000 volts or lessNo more than 3,000 hours, at least 2,000 installing
IntermediateUp to the Board limit, capped at $200,000No more than 5,750 hours, at least 5,000 installing
UnlimitedAny valueSet by Board rule
Special restricted (SP)One phase of electrical workNo more than 3,000 hours
