How does it fit the North Carolina NASCLA Commercial General Building exam?

The NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building is a national exam that North Carolina accepts in place of its own Building trade exam. A passing NASCLA score also travels to other states that accept the NASCLA exam, which makes it a good choice for contractors who bid work across state lines. The NC Business and Law exam is still required.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • NASCLA Commercial General Building exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
