How does it fit the North Carolina NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

The NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam is a national exam that the NC electrical board accepts as an alternative to its own qualifying exam. Candidates who use the NASCLA route also need at least 18 of 25 correct on the North Carolina Laws, Rules and Business Practices test, under 21 NCAC 18B .0212.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam: National exam on the NEC and contracting (Accepted in place of the Board exam)
  • NC Laws, Rules and Business Practices test: 25 questions on North Carolina rules (18 correct to pass)
