How does it fit the North Carolina Interior Construction exam?

The Interior Construction classification covers interior non-structural work such as framing, drywall, ceilings and finishes. Interior candidates study light-gauge framing, gypsum board assemblies, fire-rated partitions and the building code sections that govern them.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Interior Construction trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
