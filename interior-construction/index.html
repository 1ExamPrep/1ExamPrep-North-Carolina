What does the North Carolina Interior Construction license cover?

The Interior Construction classification covers interior non-structural work such as framing, drywall, ceilings and finishes. Interior candidates study light-gauge framing, gypsum board assemblies, fire-rated partitions and the building code sections that govern them.

Which exams do Interior Construction candidates take?

PSI gives the NCLBGC exams at its test centers. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current statutes, board rules and board pages.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
PSI Interior Construction trade examTrade knowledge, codes, estimating and safetyOpen book, 70% to pass
NC Business and Law examBusiness, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rulesOpen book, 70% to pass

How big a job can you bid with an NCLBGC license?

Every NCLBGC license carries a limitation that caps the value of any single project. A license is needed once a project costs $40,000 or more under G.S. 87-1. The limitation depends on the working capital you show, or a surety bond in its place, and the application fee set by G.S. 87-10 rises with the tier.

LimitationSingle project limitWorking capitalBond alternativeApplication fee
Limited$750,000$17,000$175,000$75
Intermediate$1,500,000$75,000$500,000$100
UnlimitedNo limit$150,000$1,000,000$125
