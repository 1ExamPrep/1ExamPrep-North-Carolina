How does it fit the North Carolina Concrete Construction exam?

The Concrete Construction classification covers foundations, slabs, structural concrete, formwork and flatwork. Expect ACI design and construction questions, reinforcing steel placement, formwork pressure and curing, with the OSHA construction rules mixed in.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Concrete Construction trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

Concrete construction involves much more than placing and finishing concrete. Contractors may need to understand structural requirements, reinforcing details, formwork systems, concrete mixture properties, curing, construction joints, sawcutting, inspection procedures, occupational safety, code administration, project management, and legal responsibilities. This package brings those subjects together in one organized study collection. Highlighted and tabbed books are especially useful for an open-book exam because candidates must often identify the correct reference and locate the applicable section under time pressure. The prepared format helps reduce unnecessary searching, but it does not replace studying. Candidates should review the books, practice locating information, and become familiar with the purpose and organization of each reference. The study-only books should be treated as learning tools. Since they are not identified as approved exam-room references, candidates should use them to understand trade concepts before the examination rather than depend on looking up every answer on test day.
