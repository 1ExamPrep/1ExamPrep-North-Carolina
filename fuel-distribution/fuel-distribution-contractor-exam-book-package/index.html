What is in the Fuel Distribution Contractor Exam Book Package?

Prepare for the North Carolina Fuel Distribution Contractor Exam with a complete reference package covering fuel-storage systems, petroleum and natural-gas piping, excavation, construction safety, fire prevention, environmental requirements, contractor law, and project operations. This package is built for candidates pursuing the North Carolina PU Fuel Distribution classification through the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors. It includes the technical codes and recommended practices allowed inside the exam room, along with additional books used to prepare for subjects that may appear on the examination but must remain outside the testing center. The PU Fuel Distribution classification covers the construction, installation, alteration, maintenance, and repair of systems used to distribute petroleum fuels, petroleum distillates, natural gas, chemicals, and slurries through pipelines from one station to another. The classification also includes excavation, trenching, and backfilling connected with this work, as well as the installation, replacement, and removal of aboveground and underground fuel-storage tanks.

How does it fit the North Carolina Fuel Distribution exam?

The Fuel Distribution subclassification covers natural gas and petroleum distribution lines and facilities. Fuel distribution questions cover pipe materials, welding, corrosion protection, testing and the federal pipeline safety rules.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Fuel Distribution trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

Fuel distribution work brings together several technical disciplines. Contractors may need to understand storage tanks, piping systems, pumps, controls, leak detection, fire prevention, pressure piping, excavation safety, environmental protection, and jobsite management. This package combines those subjects into one organized collection so candidates can prepare with the references associated with the examination. The package also includes six supporting study references that are not allowed inside the examination center. These materials address erosion and sedimentation control, excavation safety, contractor business and law, pipe and excavation operations, licensing regulations, liens, and other subjects connected with the published examination outline. Studying from the correct titles and editions can help candidates develop familiarity with each book’s organization, terminology, tables, chapters, indexes, and technical provisions.
