How does it fit the North Carolina Highway Contractor exam?

The Highway classification covers roads, streets, bridges, paving, drainage and related site work. Highway candidates study earthwork, asphalt and concrete paving, drainage structures and traffic control, plus the NCDOT standard specifications used on public road work.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Highway Contractor trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

The North Carolina Highway Contractor examination covers a wide range of construction activity. Candidates may encounter questions involving roads, bridges, drainage, grading, paving, airport surfaces, utility relocation, concrete, reinforcement, piling, tunneling, railroad construction, marine construction, traffic control, sports fields, licensing, liens, and contractor business practices. An organized reference package helps candidates prepare for this broad outline with a consistent book-navigation strategy. An open-book examination still requires thorough preparation.
