How does it fit the North Carolina Fire Alarm and Low Voltage (SP-FA/LV) exam?

The SP-FA/LV special restricted license covers fire alarm and low-voltage systems. Fire alarm candidates study NFPA 72 alongside the NEC chapters that cover limited-energy wiring.

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Fire Alarm and Low Voltage (SP-FA/LV) exam: NEC lookups, calculations and North Carolina electrical rules (Written exam given by the electrical board)
