What is in the Special Restricted Single Family Detached Residential Dwelling Contractor (SP-SFD) Exam Book Package?

The Single Family Detached Residential Dwelling classification, commonly identified as SP-SFD, is designed for electrical contractors who install, maintain, or repair wiring and electrical devices in or on qualifying single-family detached residential dwellings and their ancillary structures. The classification provides a defined residential scope and does not authorize unrestricted electrical contracting on commercial, industrial, multifamily, or attached residential properties. This package includes the 2020 National Electrical Code and NFPA 72, National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code, 2019 Edition. These technical references support preparation involving dwelling-unit services, feeders, branch circuits, receptacles, lighting outlets, appliances, grounding, bonding, smoke alarms, carbon monoxide detection interfaces, signaling equipment, fire alarm circuits, and residential electrical safety. These additional references support preparation involving North Carolina electrical contractor licensing, applicant qualifications, license classifications, project limitations, qualified-individual responsibilities, contracts, estimating, financial management, employment practices, safety, insurance, recordkeeping, project administration, and contractor compliance. Residential electrical work involves much more than installing receptacles and lighting.

How does it fit the North Carolina Single Family Detached Dwelling (SP-SFD) exam?

The SP-SFD special restricted license covers electrical work on single-family detached homes. The NC electrical board accepts the NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor exam in place of the SP-SFD exam, so both study paths are listed here.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • Board SP-SFD exam: NEC rules for single-family detached homes (Given by the electrical board)
  • NASCLA Residential Electrical Contractor exam: National residential electrical exam (Accepted in place of the SP-SFD exam)

What will you study with it?

The North Carolina SP-SFD examination is open book. The 2020 NEC and NFPA 72, 2019 Edition, are the technical exam-room references included in this package. The licensing law, Board rules, and NASCLA business reference are included for closed-book preparation and cannot be consulted during the examination. This package provides a practical group of technical, licensing, and business references for candidates preparing to demonstrate their knowledge of single-family residential electrical contracting.
