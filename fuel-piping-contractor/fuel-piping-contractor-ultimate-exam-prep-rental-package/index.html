How does it fit the North Carolina Fuel Piping Contractor exam?

The Fuel Piping Contractor license covers fuel gas piping systems. Fuel piping contractor applicants need 1 year (2,000 hours) of experience, up to 1,000 of which can be training. The exam is built on the fuel gas code.

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Fuel Piping Contractor exam: Trade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and law (Allowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers)

What will you study with it?

The technical references help candidates review fuel gas piping systems, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, pressure testing, materials, installation requirements, mechanical system provisions, and residential applications. The administrative and Board references support study of code administration, inspections, enforcement, licensing responsibilities, contractor conduct, and regulatory obligations. The NASCLA business and law reference supports preparation for business organization, project management, contracts, estimating, safety, risk management, financial management, and contractor operations.
