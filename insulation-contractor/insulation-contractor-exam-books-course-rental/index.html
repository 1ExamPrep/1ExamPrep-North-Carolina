How does it fit the North Carolina Insulation Contractor exam?

The Insulation classification covers thermal and acoustical insulation in buildings. Insulation questions cover R-values, the energy code, vapor retarders, fire ratings and installation safety.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Insulation Contractor trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
