What does the North Carolina Plumbing Contractor Class II license cover?

A Plumbing Class II license covers plumbing systems in single-family detached residential dwellings. Class II suits residential plumbers. The experience rule is the same 2 years (4,000 hours) as Class I.

Which exams do Plumbing Contractor Class II candidates take?

The Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler board runs its exams at 7 testing centers across the state. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current statutes, board rules and board pages.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
Board Plumbing Contractor Class II examTrade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and lawAllowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers

How much experience does the Plumbing Board ask for?

The Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler board lists experience by license on its applicant page. Exams are offered daily at 7 testing centers, must be taken within 90 days of notice, and a failed exam means a 90-day wait to retest.

LicenseExperience
Plumbing or heating contractor2 years (4,000 hours), up to 2,000 hours of training
Technician, Class I18 months (3,000 hours)
Technician, Class II15 months (2,500 hours)
Fuel Piping Contractor1 year (2,000 hours), up to 1,000 hours of training
Fuel Piping Technician9 months (1,500 hours)
Restricted Limited Plumbing9 months (1,500 hours), up to 750 hours of training
Fire Sprinkler Installation ContractorNICET Level III, automatic sprinkler system layout
