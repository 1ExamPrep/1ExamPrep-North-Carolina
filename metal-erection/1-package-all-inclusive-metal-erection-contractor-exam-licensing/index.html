How does it fit the North Carolina Metal Erection exam?

The Metal Erection classification covers structural steel and metal building erection. Metal erection candidates study connections, bolting, welding, rigging and the OSHA steel erection standard.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Metal Erection trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
