How does it fit the North Carolina Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor exam?

The Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor license covers the installation of fire sprinkler systems. Applicants need a current NICET Level III certificate in automatic sprinkler system layout and pass the business and law part of the Board exam.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • Business and law part of the Board exam: Contracting, business and North Carolina rules (Taken at a Board testing center)
  • NICET Level III certificate: Automatic sprinkler system layout (Required before licensing)
