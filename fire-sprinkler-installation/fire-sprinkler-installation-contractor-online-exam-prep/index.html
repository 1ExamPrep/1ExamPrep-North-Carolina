How does it fit the North Carolina Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor exam?

The Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor license covers the installation of fire sprinkler systems. Applicants need a current NICET Level III certificate in automatic sprinkler system layout and pass the business and law part of the Board exam.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • Business and law part of the Board exam: Contracting, business and North Carolina rules (Taken at a Board testing center)
  • NICET Level III certificate: Automatic sprinkler system layout (Required before licensing)

What will you study with it?

The examination component associated with this contractor license is the Board's Business and Law examination, unless the applicant qualifies for the Board's existing Business and Law examination exemption. This North Carolina Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor online exam prep course concentrates on the material candidates need for that business and law portion. The study references for this product include the NC Board's Laws & Rules - 2026 Edition and the NASCLA Business, Law, and Project Management 7th Edition, North Carolina 7th Edition. Together, these materials support preparation in state licensing rules, professional responsibilities, business organization, contracts, project management, financial practices, risk management, and other contractor business topics. For candidates with significant technical fire sprinkler experience, the business and regulatory side of licensing can require a different study approach than field work.
