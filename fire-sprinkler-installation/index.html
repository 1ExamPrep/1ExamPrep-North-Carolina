What does the North Carolina Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor license cover?

The Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor license covers the installation of fire sprinkler systems. Applicants need a current NICET Level III certificate in automatic sprinkler system layout and pass the business and law part of the Board exam.

Which exams do Fire Sprinkler Installation Contractor candidates take?

The Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler board runs its exams at 7 testing centers across the state. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current statutes, board rules and board pages.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
Business and law part of the Board examContracting, business and North Carolina rulesTaken at a Board testing center

How much experience does the Plumbing Board ask for?

The Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler board lists experience by license on its applicant page. Exams are offered daily at 7 testing centers, must be taken within 90 days of notice, and a failed exam means a 90-day wait to retest.
