How does it fit the North Carolina Unlimited Electrical Contractor (U) exam?

An Unlimited electrical license lets a contractor take any electrical project in North Carolina, regardless of value. The Unlimited exam is the hardest of the three, with heavy National Electrical Code lookups and calculations, so a tabbed and highlighted NEC pays for itself.

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Unlimited Electrical Contractor (U) exam: NEC lookups, calculations and North Carolina electrical rules (Written exam given by the electrical board)
