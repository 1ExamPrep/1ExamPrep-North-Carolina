How does it fit the North Carolina Plumbing Technician exam?

A Plumbing Technician license is for plumbers who work under a licensed contractor or for a government or institution. Technician Class I needs 18 months (3,000 hours) of experience and Class II needs 15 months (2,500 hours).

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Plumbing Technician exam: Trade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and law (Allowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers)

What will you study with it?

The North Carolina Fuel Gas Code, 2018 Edition supports preparation for fuel gas piping systems, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, pressure testing, materials, installation requirements, and safety-related fuel gas provisions. The North Carolina Administration Code and Policies, 2018 Edition supports code administration, inspection procedures, enforcement concepts, and policy-related requirements.
