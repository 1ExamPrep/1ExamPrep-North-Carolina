How does it fit the North Carolina Plumbing Technician exam?

A Plumbing Technician license is for plumbers who work under a licensed contractor or for a government or institution. Technician Class I needs 18 months (3,000 hours) of experience and Class II needs 15 months (2,500 hours).

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Plumbing Technician exam: Trade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and law (Allowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers)

What will you study with it?

For working plumbing professionals, apprentices, technicians, installers, maintenance personnel, and applicants, that flexibility can make preparation easier to manage. This course is built for candidates who want a guided study path instead of trying to work through several references without direction. The goal is to help students understand what to review, how the references support exam preparation, and how code and licensing concepts fit into the responsibilities of a plumbing technician in North Carolina. The technical side of preparation may include plumbing systems, fixtures, drainage, venting, water supply, traps, cleanouts, materials, joints, connections, fuel gas piping, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, and residential applications. The administrative and licensing side may include code administration, inspection procedures, enforcement concepts, Board laws, Board rules, technician responsibilities, and professional conduct.
