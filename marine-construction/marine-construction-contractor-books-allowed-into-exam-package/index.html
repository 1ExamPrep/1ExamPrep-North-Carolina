How does it fit the North Carolina Marine Construction exam?

The Marine Construction classification covers docks, piers, bulkheads, seawalls and other work on or over the water. With the Outer Banks, the sounds and hundreds of miles of shoreline, marine work is a real specialty in North Carolina, and the exam covers piles, timber and concrete marine structures and waterfront safety.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Marine Construction trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
