How does it fit the North Carolina Masonry Contractor exam?

The Masonry classification covers brick, block and stone construction. The masonry exam pulls from the building code, the masonry reference manuals and OSHA scaffolding rules, so tabbed books save time.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Masonry Contractor trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
