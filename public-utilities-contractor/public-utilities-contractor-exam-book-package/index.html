How does it fit the North Carolina Public Utilities Contractor exam?

The Public Utilities classification covers underground and utility construction such as water, sewer, fuel distribution and communication lines. Public Utilities has several subclassifications, and many contractors test for one of them, such as Water Lines and Sewer Lines, instead of the full classification.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Public Utilities Contractor trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

Working with the complete collection helps candidates prepare for both reference-based questions and general trade-knowledge questions. The examination may test practical understanding in addition to information found directly in the approved books, so preparation should include technical review, calculations, book navigation, and application of industry practices. The remaining ten books are included for study and must remain outside the exam room. Exam Details The North Carolina PU Public Utilities Contractor Exam is administered by PSI for the North Carolina Licensing Board for General Contractors. Candidates must receive authorization through the Board before scheduling the examination. Electrical power distribution is the largest single content area, with 15 questions. Fuel distribution, concrete and concrete reinforcement, and water and sewer piping each account for 12 questions.
