How does it fit the North Carolina Fuel Piping Technician exam?

The Fuel Piping Technician license is for gas piping technicians. Applicants need 9 months (1,500 hours) of on-site, full-time fuel piping experience.

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Fuel Piping Technician exam: Trade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and law (Allowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers)

What will you study with it?

Students can use the rental references while working through the course, reviewing important concepts, practicing reference navigation, and building a more consistent study routine.
