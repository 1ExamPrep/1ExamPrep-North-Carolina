What does the North Carolina Restricted Limited Plumbing license cover?

The Restricted Limited Plumbing license covers a narrow scope of plumbing work defined by the Board. Applicants need 9 months (1,500 hours) of experience, up to 750 hours of which can be training.

Which exams do Restricted Limited Plumbing candidates take?

The Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler board runs its exams at 7 testing centers across the state. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current statutes, board rules and board pages.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
Board Restricted Limited Plumbing examTrade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and lawAllowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers

How much experience does the Plumbing Board ask for?

The Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler board lists experience by license on its applicant page. Exams are offered daily at 7 testing centers, must be taken within 90 days of notice, and a failed exam means a 90-day wait to retest.

LicenseExperience
Plumbing or heating contractor2 years (4,000 hours), up to 2,000 hours of training
Technician, Class I18 months (3,000 hours)
Technician, Class II15 months (2,500 hours)
