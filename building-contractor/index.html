What does the North Carolina Building Contractor license cover?

The Building classification lets a North Carolina general contractor build commercial, industrial, institutional and residential buildings of any type. Building is the broadest NCLBGC classification. Candidates take the PSI Building trade exam and the Business and Law exam, and the NASCLA Commercial General Building exam is accepted in place of the Building trade exam.

Which exams do Building Contractor candidates take?

PSI gives the NCLBGC exams at its test centers. The table lists what this license needs, based on the current statutes, board rules and board pages.

ExamWhat it coversFormat
PSI Building Contractor trade examTrade knowledge, codes, estimating and safetyOpen book, 70% to pass
NC Business and Law examBusiness, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rulesOpen book, 70% to pass

How big a job can you bid with an NCLBGC license?

Every NCLBGC license carries a limitation that caps the value of any single project. A license is needed once a project costs $40,000 or more under G.S. 87-1. The limitation depends on the working capital you show, or a surety bond in its place, and the application fee set by G.S. 87-10 rises with the tier.
