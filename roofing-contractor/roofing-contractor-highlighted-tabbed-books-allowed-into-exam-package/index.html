How does it fit the North Carolina Roofing Contractor exam?

The Roofing classification covers low-slope membranes, metal panels, steep-slope roofing and flashing. The roofing exam pulls from the building code, the NRCA roofing manuals and OSHA fall protection rules, so knowing which book holds the answer matters as much as the trade knowledge.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Roofing Contractor trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
