How does it fit the North Carolina Water Purification and Sewage Disposal exam?

The Water Purification and Sewage Disposal subclassification covers treatment plants, pump stations and lift stations. Plant candidates study process equipment, concrete tankage, piping, pumps and confined-space safety.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Water Purification and Sewage Disposal trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
