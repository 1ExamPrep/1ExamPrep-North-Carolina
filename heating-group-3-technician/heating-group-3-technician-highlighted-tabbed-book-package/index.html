How does it fit the North Carolina Heating Group 3 Technician exam?

The Heating Group 3 Technician license is for forced-air HVAC technicians working under a contractor or for a government or institution. Technician Class I needs 18 months (3,000 hours) of experience and Class II needs 15 months (2,500 hours).

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Heating Group 3 Technician exam: Trade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and law (Allowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers)
