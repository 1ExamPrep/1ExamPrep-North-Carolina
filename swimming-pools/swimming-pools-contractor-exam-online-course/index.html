How does it fit the North Carolina Swimming Pools exam?

The Swimming Pools classification covers pool construction, equipment and repair. Pool candidates study construction methods, equipment, barriers and the health rules for public pools.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Swimming Pools trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
