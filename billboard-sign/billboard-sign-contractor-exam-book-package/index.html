How does it fit the North Carolina Billboard and Sign exam?

The Billboard and Sign classification covers outdoor advertising structures and large signs. Sign candidates study structural design loads, foundations, steel erection and the rules for outdoor advertising.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Billboard and Sign trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

Each publication serves a different study purpose. The North Carolina business and law guide addresses contractor responsibilities, project administration, and business practices. The concrete reference explains materials, proportioning, production, placement, and quality considerations. The reinforcing and formwork books support preparation for concrete structural work. The welding textbook provides a broad foundation in welding processes, equipment, terminology, and safe work practices. The excavation guidelines and OSHA construction standards help candidates review hazards and regulatory requirements that affect field operations. This package is especially valuable for open-book exam preparation because success requires more than owning the references. Candidates should become comfortable locating information, recognizing how each book is organized, and selecting the right publication for a question. Regular navigation practice can reduce time spent searching during the test and help candidates approach technical questions with a more consistent method.
