What does the North Carolina Journeyman Electrician license cover?

North Carolina licenses electrical contractors at the state level, and journeyman electrician credentials are handled locally or by employers. Who tests journeyman and master candidates?

North Carolina's state boards license contractors. Electrical contractor licenses come from the State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors, and plumbing contractors and technicians from the Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler board. Journeyman and master credentials for individual workers depend on the employer or the local program, so ask which code edition your exam uses before you buy. The packages here include current and older editions, clearly labeled.
