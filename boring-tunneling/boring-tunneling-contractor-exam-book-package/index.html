How does it fit the North Carolina Boring and Tunneling exam?

The Boring and Tunneling classification covers horizontal directional drilling, jack and bore and tunneling work. Boring candidates study soils, drilling fluids, casing and the 811 utility locate rules that apply before any excavation.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Boring and Tunneling trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

For the S(Boring and Tunneling) exam, the approved exam-room references are North Carolina Occupational Safety and Health Standards for the Construction Industry, with latest available amendments and Handbook of Rigging, 5th Edition, 2009. Other references associated with the examination are not permitted inside the exam room. Candidates should therefore develop a two-part preparation strategy. First, become highly familiar with the two books that can be used during the test. Second, study the major concepts represented by the non-permitted references before exam day so those subjects can be approached from knowledge rather than depending on a book lookup. This North Carolina Boring and Tunneling Contractor Exam Book Package gives candidates the approved books they can actively navigate during the examination. Familiarity with headings, indexes, tables, terminology, safety standards, lifting practices, and rigging concepts can make open-book preparation more efficient and structured.
