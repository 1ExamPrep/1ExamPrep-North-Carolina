What does the North Carolina Business and Law license cover?

The NCLBGC Business and Law exam is required of every general contractor applicant, alongside the trade exam for the classification. The exam is based on the NASCLA Contractor's Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, NC General edition, and the Board's Laws and Regulations book.
