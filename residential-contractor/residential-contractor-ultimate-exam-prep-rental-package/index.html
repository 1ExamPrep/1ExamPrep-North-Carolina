How does it fit the North Carolina Residential Contractor exam?

The Residential classification covers construction and remodeling of homes that fall under the North Carolina Residential Code. Most home builders and remodelers in North Carolina hold the Residential classification. The trade exam leans on the NC Residential Code, framing and estimating, alongside the Business and Law exam.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Residential Contractor trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
