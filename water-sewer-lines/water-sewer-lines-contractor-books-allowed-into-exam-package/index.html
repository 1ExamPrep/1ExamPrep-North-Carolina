How does it fit the North Carolina Water Lines and Sewer Lines exam?

The Water Lines and Sewer Lines subclassification covers water mains, sanitary sewers, storm drains and service lines. Line work questions cover pipe materials, bedding, pressure and leak testing, trench protection and the sedimentation rules that apply to most North Carolina excavations.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Water Lines and Sewer Lines trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

Candidates who need broader preparation may use additional study materials outside the testing center, but only the approved books should be brought to the examination appointment. An open-book contractor examination requires more than simply owning the correct references. Candidates must recognize the subject being tested, determine which book contains the applicable information, locate the correct section, and interpret technical material within the available testing time. Regular practice with the same books used on exam day can improve familiarity with their layout, terminology, illustrations, tables, headings, and installation procedures. The three-book package supports a practical preparation strategy. Use the safety standards to review jobsite hazards and worker-protection requirements. Use the ductile iron pipe guide for questions involving pipe handling, storage, joining, cutting, restraint, testing, and installation.
