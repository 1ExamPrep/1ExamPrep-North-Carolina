How does it fit the North Carolina Grading and Excavating exam?

The Grading and Excavating classification covers earthwork, excavation, site grading and erosion control. Grading questions focus on earthwork quantities, compaction, trench safety and the North Carolina Sedimentation Pollution Control Act.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Grading and Excavating trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)
