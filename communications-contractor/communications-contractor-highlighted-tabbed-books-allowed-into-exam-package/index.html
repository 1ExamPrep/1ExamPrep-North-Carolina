How does it fit the North Carolina Communications Contractor exam?

The Communications subclassification covers cable, fiber and telecommunications line construction. Communications candidates study aerial and underground plant construction, directional boring, splicing basics and utility safety.

Candidates for this license prepare for 2 exam steps:

  • PSI Communications Contractor trade exam: Trade knowledge, codes, estimating and safety (Open book, 70% to pass)
  • NC Business and Law exam: Business, contracts, lien law and NCLBGC rules (Open book, 70% to pass)

What will you study with it?

The highlighted and tabbed format helps candidates become more familiar with the structure of the safety standards before test day. Instead of searching through an unfamiliar regulatory book under time pressure, candidates can practice using clearly identified sections and highlighted passages throughout their preparation. Open-book testing still requires knowledge, organization, and time management. A candidate must identify the subject of a question, locate the applicable safety standard, read the relevant provisions, and distinguish between similar answer choices. Highlighting and tabs support this process, but candidates should still study the material and practice navigating the book repeatedly.
