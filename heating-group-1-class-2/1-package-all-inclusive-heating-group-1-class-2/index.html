How does it fit the North Carolina Heating Group 1 Class II exam?

Heating Group 1 Class II covers hydronic heating systems in single-family detached homes. The Class II exam covers the same hydronic topics as Class I with a residential focus.

Candidates for this license prepare for 1 exam step:

  • Board Heating Group 1 Class II exam: Trade knowledge, North Carolina codes, business and law (Allowed references in the room, 7 NC testing centers)
